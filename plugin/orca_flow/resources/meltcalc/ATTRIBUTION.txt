and normalizes its geometry columns and adds preset identifiers. The original
data license and notices continue to apply to those records.

It is not a burst model supplied, calibrated or endorsed by Robert Samples.
Physical heated length is used only as a filament-equivalent inventory proxy;
effective heat-transfer length is never treated as additional stored volume.
